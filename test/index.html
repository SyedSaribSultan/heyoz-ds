04

Contrast & visibility gates

Three kinds of gate, and they use different instruments on purpose. contrast — WCAG 2.x, 4.5:1 body text and 3:1 for large text and meaningful boundaries. apca — text on saturated fills, measured in Lc with a floor of 60, because WCAG 2.x gets those pairs backwards (see §05). visibility — a much lower bar, “can a human see this edge at all”, because HeyOz borders are deliberately soft and forcing 3:1 on every card edge would change the brand.
05

Text on coloured fill

white, gated on APCA
Left is what ships: white. Right is what a WCAG 2.x gate picks instead, kept here so the rejected option stays auditable — it scores the higher ratio and is plainly worse to read. WCAG 2.x has no polarity term, so its black/white crossover sits at the mid-grey #767676 and it prefers dark text on every fill lighter than that. These pairs are gated on APCA Lc 60. Read both at 14px, which is where it matters.

Interaction states

base / hover / active / disabled

08

Edges & focus rings

WCAG 1.4.11 + 2.4.11
Every border token as a real 1px rule on every surface it can sit on. Then focus rings at the real geometry (2px ring, 2px offset). A ring on a brand-filled button uses border/focus-inverse, which is the only value that clears 3:1 there.
09

Elevation

4 steps + scrim
Light shadows are a warm tinted grey rather than black so they read as shadow instead of dirt. Dark shadows go black and lean harder on surface + border, because a black shadow on a near-black page has nowhere to darken to.

Chart series

6 series, per-mode
Series are staggered in lightness as well as hue, so they survive the Greyscale toggle. The shipped --chart-1..5 were byte-identical in light and dark and clustered in one lightness band.
